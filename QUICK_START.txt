🚀 EASIEST WAY: Vercel Dashboard (No Code!)

1. Go to: https://vercel.com
2. Sign up with email or GitHub
3. Click "Add New Project"
4. Click "Continue with GitHub" and connect your repo
   OR drag/drop the /app/frontend folder
5. Vercel auto-detects React → Click "Deploy"
6. Wait 2 minutes → Get your URL!

   Your URL will be: https://keyboard-game-xxx.vercel.app

🌐 CONNECT YOUR GODADDY DOMAIN

After deploying to Vercel:

In Vercel:
1. Go to Project Settings → Domains
2. Add your domain (e.g., game.yourdomain.com)
3. Copy the DNS records shown

In GoDaddy:
1. Login → My Products → Your Domain → DNS
2. Add these records:

   Type: CNAME
   Name: game (or keyboard, or whatever you want)
   Value: cname.vercel-dns.com
   TTL: 600

3. Save → Wait 10-60 minutes

Result: https://game.yourdomain.com 🎉
